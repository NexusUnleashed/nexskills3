## Core Design Principles

This is an in development application, we are still in development. There are no existing users or legacy ties, we do not need to concern ourselves with backwards compatability. Our decisions should be free and pure driven only by the goal of the ideal architecture in keeping with our design principles.

The only valid decision criterion is the optimal end-state solution. Time, implementation effort, refactor cost, migration complexity, disruption, scope of rewrite, and transition risk are never reasons to weaken a recommendation. If the best solution requires replacing whole subsystems or restructuring every layer, recommend that directly.

Optimize every design decision for:

- Efficiency
- Performance
- Functionality
- Clarity
- Elegance
- Maintainability
- Separation of concerns
- Simplicity
